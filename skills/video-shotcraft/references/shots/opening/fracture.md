summary: 5×5 tiles gather from a 3D shattered state into a whole poster, ring by ring in a ripple from the centre; after a beat with the lettering lit, all the fragments accelerate away from the centre, spinning, and fly off screen
use_when: The first shot of an opening, a brand/poster reveal going "from chaos to form"; played in reverse, or using only the second half, it works as a hard transition
duration: about 5.2s (156f@30fps; assemble 0–2.6s · hold 1s · scatter 1.5s)
energy: High (high energy at both ends, still in the middle, suited to cutting in and out on BGM accents)

## Intent
Film "the product taking shape" as a physical event: fragments fly back into place from three-dimensional space, each with its own spin, and the audience watches a complete picture grow out of disorder; after the hold to read the text, the whole thing then bursts off screen, handing attention cleanly to the next shot. Entrance and exit are symmetrical, so one piece of footage can be used in both directions.

## Motion core
- Each of the 25 tiles holds its own deterministic pseudo-random initial pose: offset ±450/±300/±490px (x/y/z) + ±180° rotation on all three axes, generated by `rand(seed)` so the render is reproducible
- The entrance is staggered as a ripple by Manhattan distance: `delay = (|r-2|+|c-2|) * 0.045`, the centre settles first and the four corners last, each tile takes 0.34 of duration on `bezier(0.42,0,0.3,1)` (a long deceleration before landing); about 10f after landing, one damped rotateX wobble of ≤2.5° = the "click" of slotting in
- The 25 tiles are cut from **the same poster** (each tile takes its own cell by grid-position offset), and the complete picture is only seen once they are assembled; tiles are shaded by facing direction (the more side-on to the camera, the darker)
- Opacity rises to 1 quickly at `tin * 2.5` — the fragments are nearly solid even from afar, avoiding a "fade-in feel" that would dilute the physicality
- The poster is printed with the eyebrow INTRODUCING + the video-shotcraft wordmark (mark + lowercase wordmark), sliced along with the tiles, lighting up at t=0.42–0.52 to hold the eye through the hold section; the exit does not fade it out separately — the text and mark are torn away along with the fragments; during the hold the whole poster pushes in very slowly by 3%
- The exit does not fade out: each tile flies 620–880px out along the direction away from the frame centre (computed by `atan2` + ±0.8 jitter) while adding ±150° of spin, accelerating on `inCubic`, leaving the frame as a solid object

## Parameters
| Parameter | Typical value | Tuning feel |
|------|--------|----------|
| Grid | 5×5, 380×230px, perspective 900px | More tiles make the ripple finer but weaken each tile's presence; perspective <700 exaggerates the perspective and easily breaks the illusion |
| Initial scatter | dx±450 / dy±300 / dz −210~+490 | A larger scatter makes the opening more chaotic, but the assembly path gets longer and the delay window must be increased to match |
| Ripple interval | 0.045 per ring (Manhattan distance) | >0.07 the ripple turns into "roll-calling ring by ring"; <0.03 everything lands nearly at once and the ripple is lost |
| Entrance duration | 0.34 per tile (inOutCubic) | Shortening it makes the form snap in more sharply; together with the ripple interval, make sure the last tile settles by ≤0.6 |
| Exit window | Takes off at 0.70 + delay×0.5, 0.18 duration (inCubic) | **The window must close before t≈0.97**: 0.72+delay×0.7/0.26 once left the corner tiles only partly off screen, stuck at the frame edge for a second (precedent 2026-08-05) |
| Exit distance | 620–880px (away from the centre + random jitter) | Too short a distance leaves them lingering at the frame edge; the centre tile's direction is random (there is no "away from" to speak of) |
| Poster colour scheme | `ACCENT_HUE=218` derives the whole poster (cool dark stage + upper-left soft light + glowing horizon arc at the bottom) | Change the one hue constant and the whole poster changes colour; seams 0.5 design px, any wider and the lettering loses strokes |

## Known pitfalls
- The exit leaves the frame by displacement rather than opacity fade — a fade reads as "vanishing" rather than "flying away" and loses all physicality; when changing parameters keep `opacity = min(1, tin*2.5)` with no exit term attached
- All random quantities must go through the deterministic `rand(seed)` (equivalent to Remotion `random()`); true randomness makes renders jitter between runs
- The hold section (t≈0.6–0.7) is the only window for reading the text; when lengthening the copy, widen the gap between the label fade-out and the first tile's take-off to match, otherwise the tiles scatter before the text is read
- The mark must not straddle a seam: a seam cuts the mark with a thin gap/ragged edge, which reads as a broken image. The demo centres the wordmark on the centre of the row-3 tile and sets the font size to 22 so the mark falls entirely within the column-2 tile, so when the fragments scatter the mark flies away whole with one tile; when changing the mark size or the grid, recompute together
- When used as a transition, take only the t=0.7–1 scatter section, half played in reverse and half forward; pasting footage from the two sides onto the tile faces needs separate texture mapping
